Below is a **pencil-and-paper dry-run** that walks through every arithmetic step of TRUST-MCNet when **two clients ( C₁, C₂ )** train for **two communication rounds**.
We pick very small, round numbers so every calculation can be followed by eye.

---

## 0 Initial set-up

| Item                                | Value we assume                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Global model before training        | $W^{0}=[0,\;0]$                                              |
| Initial hybrid weights              | $\alpha^{0}=0.40,\; \beta^{0}=0.30,\; \gamma^{0}=0.30$       |
| Reputation seed                     | $\operatorname{rep}_{1}^{0}=\operatorname{rep}_{2}^{0}=0.50$ |
| Reputation decay                    | $\lambda = 0.90$                                             |
| Learning-rate for weight-adaptation | $\eta = 0.05$                                                |
| Trust threshold (soft filter)       | $\tau = 0.20$                                                |

*(Entropy is already mapped so “large = good”; no inversion step is needed)*

---

## 1 Round 1

### 1.1 Clients send their updates

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta w_1^{1} &= [\;1,\; 2\;] \\[2pt]
\Delta w_2^{1} &= [-1,\; 1\;]
\end{aligned}
$$

Mean update (reference vector)
$\bar{\Delta w}^{\,1}=([1,2]+[-1,1])/2=[\,0,\;1.5]$.

### 1.2 Cosine similarity

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{cos}_1^{1}
&=\frac{[1,2]\!\cdot[0,1.5]}{\|[1,2]\|\;\|[0,1.5]\|}
   =\frac{3}{\sqrt5\,(1.5)} \approx 0.895\\[6pt]
\operatorname{cos}_2^{1}
&=\frac{[-1,1]\!\cdot[0,1.5]}{\sqrt2\,(1.5)}
   =\frac{1.5}{2.121}\approx 0.707
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.3 Entropy on probe set

(assign plausible trust-like scores)

$$
\operatorname{ent}_1^{1}=0.80,
\qquad
\operatorname{ent}_2^{1}=0.40
$$

### 1.4 Reputation after **0** rounds

$$
\operatorname{rep}_1^{1}=0.50,
\qquad
\operatorname{rep}_2^{1}=0.50
$$

### 1.5 Hybrid trust scores

(using $\alpha^{0},\beta^{0},\gamma^{0}$)

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_1^{1}&=0.4(0.895)+0.3(0.80)+0.3(0.50)=0.748\\
T_2^{1}&=0.4(0.707)+0.3(0.40)+0.3(0.50)=0.553
\end{aligned}
$$

Both exceed $\tau$; no one is dropped.

Weights for aggregation

$$
w_1^{1}
=\frac{0.748}{0.748+0.553}=0.575,
\qquad
w_2^{1}=0.425
$$

### 1.6 Aggregate and update the model

$$
\Delta W^{1}
=0.575\,[1,2]+0.425\,[-1,1]
=[\,0.15,\; 1.575]
$$

$$
W^{1}=W^{0}+\Delta W^{1}=[\,0.15,\;1.575]
$$

### 1.7 Measure each client’s contribution

Assume **global accuracy** rises from 70 % to:

* 76 % if only C₁’s update were applied ⇒ $\Delta\!{\rm Acc}_1^{1}=+0.06$
* 72 % with only C₂ ⇒ $\Delta\!{\rm Acc}_2^{1}=+0.02$

### 1.8 Spearman correlations (two-client shortcut)

For each metric, compare *which client ranks higher*;
if the ranking matches that of $\Delta\!{\rm Acc}$ ⇒ ρ = +1,
if opposite ⇒ ρ = -1, tie ⇒ 0.

| Metric     | Ranking         | Matches ΔAcc ranking? | ρ  |
| ---------- | --------------- | --------------------- | -- |
| Cosine     | C₁ (0.895) > C₂ | ✔                     | +1 |
| Entropy    | C₁ (0.80) > C₂  | ✔                     | +1 |
| Reputation | C₁=C₂           | tie                   | 0  |

$\rho^{1}=[+1,\; +1,\; 0]$.

### 1.9 Update the hybrid coefficients

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha^{1}&=0.40+0.05(+1)=0.45\\
\beta^{1}&=0.30+0.05(+1)=0.35\\
\gamma^{1}&=0.30+0.05(0)=0.30
\end{aligned}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad\text{normalise}\;\;(\Sigma=1.10)
$$

$$
\boxed{\,
\alpha^{1}=0.409,\;
\beta^{1}=0.318,\;
\gamma^{1}=0.273
\,}
$$

### 1.10 Roll the reputation forward

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rep}_1^{2}
&=0.9(0.50)+0.1(0.06)=0.456\\
\operatorname{rep}_2^{2}
&=0.9(0.50)+0.1(0.02)=0.452
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 2 Round 2  (using the **new** weights)

### 2.1 New client updates

$$
\Delta w_1^{2}=[\;0.5,\;1.0\;],\qquad
\Delta w_2^{2}=[-0.2,\;0.4]
$$

Reference vector
$\bar{\Delta w}^{\,2}=[0.15,\;0.7]$.

### 2.2 Cosine similarity

$$
\operatorname{cos}_1^{2}\approx 0.967,\qquad
\operatorname{cos}_2^{2}\approx 0.781
$$

### 2.3 Entropy and reputation

$$
\operatorname{ent}_1^{2}=0.85,\;
\operatorname{ent}_2^{2}=0.50
$$

$$
\operatorname{rep}_1^{2}=0.456,\;
\operatorname{rep}_2^{2}=0.452
$$

### 2.4 Hybrid trust with $(\alpha^{1},\beta^{1},\gamma^{1})$

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_1^{2}&=0.409(0.967)+0.318(0.85)+0.273(0.456)=0.791\\
T_2^{2}&=0.409(0.781)+0.318(0.50)+0.273(0.452)=0.601
\end{aligned}
$$

Weights
$w_1^{2}=0.791/(0.791+0.601)=0.568,\;
  w_2^{2}=0.432$.

### 2.5 Aggregate

$$
\Delta W^{2}
=0.568[0.5,1]+0.432[-0.2,0.4]
=[\,0.206,\; 0.705]
$$

$$
W^{2}=W^{1}+\Delta W^{2}
=[\,0.356,\;2.280]
$$

### 2.6 New contributions & correlations

Suppose single-update accuracies are now:

* with C₁ only → 78 % ⇒ $\Delta\!{\rm Acc}_1^{2}=+0.03$,
* with C₂ only → 76 % ⇒ $\Delta\!{\rm Acc}_2^{2}=+0.01$.

Ranking again matches cos, ent **and** the tiny rep difference,
so $\rho^{2}=[+1,\; +1,\; +1]$.

### 2.7 Coefficient update #2

$$
\alpha^{2}=0.409+0.05=0.459,\;
\beta^{2}=0.318+0.05=0.368,\;
\gamma^{2}=0.273+0.05=0.323
\;\Rightarrow\;\text{normalise}(\Sigma=1.15)
$$

$$
\boxed{\,
\alpha^{2}=0.399,\;
\beta^{2}=0.320,\;
\gamma^{2}=0.281
\,}
$$

After only two rounds the system has **automatically increased the weight on cosine (alignment) and entropy (confidence)** while letting reputation shrink slightly, exactly mirroring which signals best predicted helpful updates.

---

### 3 What the dry run shows

* **Trust weights really do move**: from *(0.40, 0.30, 0.30)* → *(0.409, 0.318, 0.273)* → *(0.399, 0.320, 0.281)*.
* **Aggregation weights favour the better client** (C₁) more strongly in round 2 (0.568 vs 0.432).
* Even with only **two clients** the Spearman-sign trick is enough to steer the coefficients, because every round still yields a clear “which client helped more” signal.

You can plug these very same arithmetic steps into a unit-test to confirm your implementation of the adaptive-weight logic.
